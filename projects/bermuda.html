// The brief

Sell an experience you can't put in a product shot.

Bermuda is an event agency from Berchem where creativity is limitless and every event is a new concept. The site had to convey feeling — atmosphere, scale, trust — and then convert that into qualified enquiries.

The build leans on a "Get Lost In" motif — Creativity, Details, Personality — past productions framed at premium scale, and a contact funnel designed to qualify rather than just collect. Built bilingual NL · EN.

Why it's recolored here

Live, Bermuda runs on its own earth-tone identity — clay, sand, olive. For this case study I remapped the exact same morph to the portfolio's own system: neutral ink at rest, brightening to full contrast on focus, with the morphing focal shape resolving in the accent. The geometry is untouched — only the palette is swapped to black / white / accent so the component sits inside this site instead of fighting it.
