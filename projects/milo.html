// The brief

A body of work, not a grid of thumbnails.

Milo's practice moves between digital and analogue, documentary and directed. A flat gallery would flatten that range. Instead the site reads like a book of chapters — Set, Corporate & Brand, Events, Portraits, Product & Food, Fine Art — each with its own voice.

A dark, cinematic shell lets the photography glow, while sticky chapter navigation keeps you oriented across 55 projects. Trilingual EN · NL · FR, with strong Lighthouse scores on a deeply image-heavy site.
